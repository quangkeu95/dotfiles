# External dependencies and redemption

**Trigger:** advertised backing, utility, reward value, or an exit depends on another
asset, vault, bridge, oracle, keeper, API, custodian, lending system or issuer.

**Minimum evidence:** exact dependency contracts/chains/pins, runtime/source/control
provenance, inventory and liabilities, share/claim conversion rules, claimant rights,
received asset, approvals, caps, timing, fees, escrow/encumbrances, and exit execution
or explicitly limited previews. For offchain rights preserve authoritative terms
and eligibility plus evidence that the operational route exists.

Draw the shortest asset/control chain from holder entitlement to intended underlying.
Distinguish a synthetic claim, accounting receipt, vault share, bond/NFT, wrapped
asset, and actual liquid underlying. Check who can redeem, how many units result,
who can change conversion or eligibility, collateral priority and competing claims,
liquidation/oracle assumptions, pause/upgrade/keeper dependencies and transfer rules.
A vault balance or published peg does not establish redeemable backing.
Bridge/custody risks need independent chain evidence and the relevant legal or
operational terms; code alone cannot establish issuer obligations.

For a decisive fork redemption or sale require successful receipt plus actual
underlying balance increase net of explained costs and route. A success event,
synthetic token receipt, previewRedeem return or balance in an unrelated vault is
insufficient. Test relevant size/caps/time assumptions without real transactions.

**Stop:** enforceable rights, material dependencies and realistic route are bounded;
or a missing dependency/underlying exit controls the verdict.
**Unfinished means:** backing/redemption remains unproven even if the immutable token
and canonical LP have no detected control paths. Route substantial protocol-wide
invariant analysis to a separate audit scope.
