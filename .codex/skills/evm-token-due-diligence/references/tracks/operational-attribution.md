# Narrow operational attribution

**Trigger:** a material authority, fee recipient or project-control statement cannot
be answered without attributing a public operational role. Do not start a broad
personal-identity investigation by default.

**Minimum evidence:** authenticated project publication tied to exact addresses,
onchain authorization/control evidence, signed public attestations when independently
verifiable, deployment/role-change transactions, and provenance/time of each claim.
Project labels are claims to corroborate, not identity proof.

Use launch signer, direct buy recipient, funder, fee recipient, keeper, observed
controller, or project-published address as appropriate. Separate signing authority,
beneficial ownership and human identity. Shared exchange funding, routers, settlement
destinations, timing, deterministic addresses or common hosting do not alone prove
common ownership, coordination, intent or fraud. Do not ask for a new signature or
private key to establish a role; use existing authenticated evidence.

Map only necessary control links. Preserve alternatives such as service accounts,
custodians, sponsored gas, OTC settlement, and relays. Stop exact flow attribution at
commingling. Describe verified sale/rebuy routing as market-mediated redistribution
unless independently stronger evidence supports a stronger proposition.

**Stop:** the operational role needed by the decision is established or is explicitly
unknown. No expansion into family, personal contacts or speculative identity.
**Unfinished means:** findings attach to addresses/roles, not a named individual.
Deliver narrow propositions with alternatives, confidence and stale conditions.
